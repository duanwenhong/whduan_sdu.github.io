Wenhong Duan

I am an Assistant Professor and master's supervisor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shandong University, which I joined in July 2025. I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Wen Gao and Prof. Siwei Ma.

My research focuses on intelligent image and video coding, including learned image and video compression, intelligent visual coding, and 3D visual representation and compression. I also work on model compression, and my recent interests include embodied AI and multimodal compression.

I lead a project funded by the Young Scientists Fund of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and have published more than ten papers in journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Image Processing and IEEE Transactions on Multimedia. I have contributed to five video coding standards, submitted 30 technical proposals to AVS and its working groups, and maintain reference software for video coding standards. Results from this work have been applied in ByteDance's intelligent video coding platform. I received IEEE Outstanding Awards in 2022 and 2024 and was selected for the Chinese Institute of Electronics Young Talent Support Program in 2026.

I welcome prospective graduate students in computer science and technology and artificial intelligence, particularly those from related information science and engineering disciplines. I also welcome undergraduate research interns year-round.
